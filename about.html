ABOUT

Miles V Soldier writes dispatches — short, compressed pieces that sit somewhere between poetry, philosophy and reportage. Each line stands alone on its own page, set in capitals, so that every sentence has to carry its full weight before the reader turns to the next.

The language is deliberately dense. Terms from physics, neuroscience, psychology, navigation, military doctrine, law and finance collide with Latin, Japanese, Irish, Arabic, Hebrew and Igbo, and with the vocabulary of alchemy and the sea. A single dispatch can move from Spinoza to the ocean floor, from a sumo ring to a prison cell, from Hiroshima to the New Mexico desert. Each book closes with a full glossary, with sources for its historical and scientific references, so the reader can follow any thread as far as they wish.

The work returns again and again to the same questions: what consciousness is and where meaning comes from; how individuals and nations betray themselves; what war, empire, slavery and propaganda do to the human spirit; and how people endure — through confession, compassion, faith, resistance and the long work of becoming whole.

The four books of The Miles V Soldier Collection were written over almost fifteen years. They are printed in Canada.
